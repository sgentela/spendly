Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account with Spendly, we collect basic information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password (stored in hashed form).

When you use the Service, we collect the expense data you log — including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions — so we can display it back to you and produce summaries.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to provide and improve the Service. This includes authenticating you, storing your expense records, generating category breakdowns and monthly summaries, and communicating important account-related notices.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your financial data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on servers operated on our behalf and is protected with industry-standard safeguards. Passwords are never stored in plain text.

You may request export or deletion of your data at any time by contacting us. When you delete your account, your expense records are removed from active systems within a reasonable period.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly may rely on a small number of third-party providers for infrastructure such as hosting and email delivery. These providers process data only as needed to support the Service and are bound by their own privacy and security commitments.

We do not share your expense data with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics platforms that profile individual users.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at privacy@spendly.example.
